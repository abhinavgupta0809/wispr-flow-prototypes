Journal Mode

When the words are the point.

Capture your thoughts in your own words. Clean them up when you're ready.

Who it's for

The voice journaler

Speaks to think. Wants their own words kept, not polished into someone else's.

  • 101complaints about AI editing; some say cleanup changed their meaning
  • Mostlyhow well Flow kept my voice in my own journal test

Why it matters. Journaling happens away from work. A keep-it-raw switch could give people a reason to open Flow outside the workday (my assumption).

A preference to test, not a fix. An archetype from public reviews and my own use.

Problem

When you journal by voice, you may want your own words kept. There's no “keep it raw, just this once” switch.

Hypothesis In my test Flow mostly kept my voice. This is about choice, not a fix.

Solution

Turn on Journal Mode, speak, keep your words. Polish later if you want.

    Why this, and what my test showedA preference hypothesis
    Preference hypothesis

    When people journal or think aloud, some would rather keep the way they said it: the hedges, the restarts, the word they'd never write. Then decide later whether to tidy it.

    My testNot yet verified

    In my journal comparison, the output largely kept my tone and the story's details. So this isn't a claim that Flow strips personality. Evidence record R2
