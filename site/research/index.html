What my own tests showed

I started by testing whether cleanup drops what people say. Mostly it didn't. The tests did show two smaller things worth designing for: choices I changed mid-sentence, and a word Flow misheard. That's how Thought Check became Review Revisions.

What I've found so far

  • The ten controlled passages largely kept their substantive meaning.
  • The journal comparison largely kept my tone and the story's details.
  • Several spoken corrections came out right.
  • In my second guest-list test, I meant to keep Leo and drop Sam. The output kept both. (In the first test, adding Sam back was on purpose.)
  • A complex scheduling dictation kept the final date and time, but wrote “the smaller group” without saying who that was.
  • Flow heard “Mobile Fix Strip” as “mobile fix trip.”

How to read these

One speaker, my own Flow app. These are my readings of my own runs, reported Oct 16, 2026. They aren't Flow's error rate, and none has been checked against its recording yet. Each record says which proposal it informs.

To finish a record, paste the actual output, write what I actually said from the recording, and mark it verified.

Method

  1. Set the conditions below once. Keep device, microphone, app version and cleanup settings the same for all ten runs.
  2. Read each passage aloud into the Flow app. Record the audio separately (voice memo or screen recording).
  3. Paste Flow's output into Actual Flow output without editing it, then lock it.
  4. If you said something different from the script, mark it and write what you actually said. Judge the run against that.
  5. If Flow shows a pre-cleanup transcript, paste it too. If not, leave it empty: discrepancies are then end-to-end.
  6. Mark each meaning unit. Rewording, removed fillers and legitimate self-corrections are not errors.
  7. After all ten, repeat each passage that needed substantive correction, to see if it recurs.
  8. Then record the two unscripted brain dumps and assess them against their recordings.

This workspace never transcribes anything. Every output here is pasted from the real app.

Where this started

Reviewers said cleanup changed what they meant

These reviews led to the first hypothesis: that people may not notice when a cleaned-up draft drops a detail or a condition. My runs mostly didn't show that, so I replaced the omission check (Thought Check). The reviews still show the worry exists for some people. They don't show how often it happens.
